Tip Jar Privacy Policy

Effective November 2026

Tip Jar is developed by Little Wins Development.

Information stored

Tip Jar currently saves shift dates, hours, tip amounts, tip-outs, tip-ins, hourly wages, and optional notes locally in your browser or installed web app on your device. This data is not sent to Little Wins Development servers by the current web app.

Export and restore

You may export your shift data as a CSV or JSON backup. Any file you choose to download, store, or share is under your control. Restoring a backup replaces local shift records.

Storage and deletion

Deleting site data or uninstalling the app may remove local records. Export a backup before clearing data. You may delete individual shifts within the app.

Purchases

The current web app does not process payments. A future Google Play release may offer optional paid features using Google Play Billing. This policy will be updated before that release to describe any additional data processing.

Children

Tip Jar is designed for workers tracking earnings, not specifically for children.

Changes

We may update this policy as features change. The current version will be available on this page.
