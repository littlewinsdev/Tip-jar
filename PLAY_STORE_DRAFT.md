# Tip Jar — Google Play listing draft

**App name:** Tip Jar — Shift & Tip Tracker

**Short description:** Track shifts, tip-outs, hourly wages, and what you really earn.

**Full description:**
Tip Jar is a simple shift and tip tracker built for servers, bartenders, and other tipped workers.

• See your shifts on an easy-to-read monthly calendar.
• Track cash tips, card tips, tip-outs, and tip-ins.
• Calculate net tips, tips per hour, and combined hourly earnings.
• Review weekly and monthly totals.
• Keep your shift history on your device.

Free core tracking. Optional Pro features are planned for launch; do not advertise as available until implemented and tested.

**Planned one-time product:** tip_jar_pro — $2.99 (US), non-consumable.

**Planned Pro features:** saved automatic tip-out rules, advanced reports, CSV export, custom reporting periods.
